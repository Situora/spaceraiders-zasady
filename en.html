SpaceRaiders – privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026.

The operator is the developer named in the SpaceRaiders listing on Google Play.

SpaceRaiders does not collect or share personal or gameplay data. The game uses no network connection and contains no ads, analytics, player accounts or real-money purchases.

Data on your device

Game progress and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The game does not transmit them anywhere. Cloud backup for the app is disabled. Vibration permission provides haptic feedback.

To delete local data, open Android Settings → Apps → SpaceRaiders → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game. This removes your saved progress and settings. The developer has no server copy.

Distribution and contact

Google handles distribution and updates through Google Play under its own terms. This policy describes the SpaceRaiders game itself.

Contact karlovsky.apps@gmail.com with questions. If you choose to email us, we use your message and email address to respond to your enquiry; please do not send sensitive information.
